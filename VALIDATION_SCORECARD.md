## Criterion 1 — Pattern Discovery Quality
**Pass threshold:** AI surfaces at least 2 agent-level 
patterns involving a combination of 3+ variables 
(e.g. AHT + QA score + day-of-week). Each pattern 
must be stated as a specific predictive claim before 
reviewing outcomes — written down and timestamped. 
At least 1 confirmed by outcome data within 60 days.

## Criterion 2 — Intervention Impact
**Pass threshold:** AI identifies a specific agent 
and behavioral pattern before any coaching occurs. 
Target metric moves by at least 10% on AHT or ACW, 
or at least 5 points on Balto QA score within 30 days, 
measured against that agent's own 30-day baseline. 
AI prediction must match direction and approximate 
magnitude of what actually moved.

## Criterion 3 — Pipeline Efficiency
**Pass threshold:** Full monthly briefing completes 
in under 4 hours. Validated on a second distinct 
data configuration, not just the test environment. 
Variance between runs under 30 minutes. Effective 
hourly rate clears $150/hr at target price point 
after all production time.

## Criterion 4 — Insight Consistency Across Time
**Pass threshold:** At least 60% of Month 1 actionable 
patterns either confirmed by Month 2 data or have a 
documented reason they resolved or shifted. 
Undocumented disappearance counts as a failure.

## Criterion 5 — Client-Readiness of Output
**Pass threshold:** One person with relevant domain 
context reviews the briefing cold with no prior 
explanation. Must correctly identify without prompting: 
the top priority action, which agent or team it applies 
to, and what success looks like. All three required to pass.
